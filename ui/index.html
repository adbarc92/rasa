    Find and redact

    Matches every instance, ignoring case, and covers the whole word it appears in ("Smith" removes all of "jsmith@mail.com"). Names vary, so add each form: "Alex Barclay" won't find "Alexander Barclay", but "Barclay" finds both.

      Terms stay in place when you open another file or folder.

      Manual boxes

      Drag on a page to cover anything search missed, such as signatures, pictures or logos. Click a box to remove it. Ctrl+Z undoes the last box.
